## Server contract
`getCommercialMonth("YYYY-MM")` in `services/commercial-history.ts` requires the existing admin session before constructing the server client. It is server-only, not a Server Action/route, and does not cache across users. Future pages must retain their own guards. Do not expose the internal page reader as an API endpoint.

`MonthReport` in `lib/commercial-reporting/contracts.ts` contains:
- `sales`: one fact per canonical orders ID in the **registration/source month**, including excluded facts with explicit reasons for admin diagnosis. `origin` is physical_historical, physical_current, online or other. Original channel, date, price, subtotal, discount and total remain under `original`; lines retain all original snapshots. Do NOT label historical subtotal as a verified original list price: that contract preserved total as subtotal and discount0.
- `purchases`: source/current purchase month, recorded amounts, original lines, provenance, date quality and recognized/excluded flags. Drafts/cancellations stay visible but do not contribute to acquisition totals. These are purchases, NOT certified cash outflows to suppliers.
- `datedReceipts`: individual approved payment entries whose **paid_at month** matches the request, including receipts belonging to an older sale. The parent cancellation state remains visible; cancelling an order does not prove a refund occurred.
- `undatedReceiptsAttributedToSales`: approved receipts without paid_at linked to sales in the selected source/registration month. Their paidAt and occurredOn remain NULL. This is attribution, never a fabricated cash date or import-time date.
- `totals`: integer ARS cents, separately registeredSales, confirmedSales, approvedPaymentsForSales, pendingForSales, datedReceipts, undatedApprovedForSales, purchases, imputedPurchases and unknownDatePurchases. Several columns have different temporal bases and are **not** intended to be added together. The undated amount is already part of payments for sales, not extra income.
- `paymentMethodsForSales` versus `paymentMethodsForDatedReceipts`: distinct semantic scopes. Amount comes from each payment ledger entry; detailed store_payment_method overrides coarse method only when actually stored. Old card remains unspecified card; other does not become QR. Distinct orders per method are not additive across methods.
- `lineCosts`: stored unit cost in cents or NULL plus evidence classification. NULL stays unknown; zero remains stored zero but is not certified without separate evidence. Positive stored snapshots with the published atomic-sale cost policy have transaction-snapshot provenance; this does NOT independently certify supplier invoice accuracy. Current product costs are never read or used to fill gaps.
- Partial gross margin is available only on confirmed, line/header-reconciled sales and only for lines with traceable snapshots. It is not overall profit: unknown lines, fees, operating expenses and unverified refunds are outside coverage. A lack of cost returns NULL, never a zero margin.
- `issues`, `warnings`, `provisional`, `consistency`: must remain visible to future consumers. Do not silently present a provisional report as certified or turn read errors into zero cards.

## Inclusion rules and integrity
Confirmed sale requires a noncancelled positive amount, an existing paid/completed indication AND approved payments exactly equal to total cents; refunded states are sent to review. A completed header without approved money is not confirmed income. Approved receipts are separately observable even on a cancelled parent. Refund events/partial amounts are not reconstructed from status; reported ledger sums are not certified net of refunds.

No SQL joins between payment and item arrays: each is grouped independently by order ID, so multiple payments and products cannot multiply sale totals. Duplicate IDs, ambiguous historical provenance, orphan relations and forbidden historical inventory flags fail rather than silently deduplicating/filtering them away. Historical source line totals are respected when unit_price*quantity differs. A line/header mismatch is surfaced and disables margin, not automatically fixed or allocated.

All amounts use exact cents, reject excessive precision/invalid money and check safe integer sums. Original values are returned without rounding, repricing or rediscounting. No stock/current-cost fields are selected. Read SDK operations are GET SELECT only; no commercial RPCs, storage calls or background jobs.

## Dates
`America/Argentina/Buenos_Aires` is explicit. Source DATE values are validated/preserved as civil dates. Operational order timestamps and real paid_at use the IANA zone. `monthBounds()` provides inclusive start/exclusive next-month start via IANA conversion, not hardcoded UTC-3. The period projection currently loads the compact complete read dataset then selects periods; this intentionally also finds payments this month for older orders.

`evidence.ts` binds documented/imputed historical quality to batch ID AND certified source SHA-256 plus ledger sheet/row. Only rows154..159 of that certified purchase batch are imputed. A missing/changed batch or missing ledger gives unknown provenance, never a new invented date. No stored historical date is changed. Evidence is versioned and is not applied automatically to other spreadsheets/lots.

## Synthetic exclusions
The manifest includes only nonpersonal exact fixture identifiers from both existing acceptance registries: the abandoned draft fixture and the completed paid acceptance. A matching operation, product or category excludes the whole operation and associated receipts from commercial sums, even if the product is archived/draft or mixed with other lines. Historical/archived products in general are NOT excluded. Excluded facts remain available for admin diagnosis and records are never deleted.

No customer identifiers, credentials or private reports are embedded. Coverage is deliberately `known_fixtures_only`; it does not claim to inventory future/unknown tests. Extend only from reviewed evidence. Future UI must display provisional coverage until an explicit complete exclusion review is available. Historical monetary evidence was confirmed in Phase2A; this code did not reconnect to Production to repeat it.

## Completeness, limits and read consistency
The read adapter selects eight fixed compact table projections (including ledger/batch provenance), with UUID keyset ordering by id and no hardcoded100-record result cap. It continues after short pages to tolerate PostgREST server clamps; only an empty page ends a table. Out-of-order/duplicate pages fail. A30-second total budget,15-second per-HTTP timeout and10000-page per-table safety limit fail the report entirely; they never return successful partial totals.

Multiple SELECTs are **not a database transaction snapshot**. Report consistency explicitly states best_effort_multi_select_not_transaction_snapshot. Relation checks detect some concurrent changes but do not prove global consistency; refresh after operations settle for reconciliation. Current first-stage volume permits full compact scans; substantially larger data should move to a separately reviewed read-only aggregate/snapshot strategy, without reverting to summing paginated UI rows. No such schema change is included here.

## Phase2B.3 — monthly commercial Analytics

`/admin/analytics?month=YYYY-MM` now uses the existing commercial projection, with a native month/year selector and Argentina's IANA timezone. The default is the current commercial month, never the server's local month. Invalid or repeated month parameters show an explicit error, rather than silently choosing a different period. The route has its own administrative session guard, a loading state and sanitized errors with no partial/zero-success report.

`getCommercialAnalytics(month)` is server-only and guards before constructing the privileged client. It validates the period, reads one complete compact paginated snapshot using the same SELECT whitelist as `getCommercialMonth`, then calls `buildCommercialFacts` once. `buildCommercialMonthFromFacts` supplies the exact same MonthReport contract to the selected month, previous month and six-month evolution; it avoids six repeated database scans. Existing getCommercialMonth semantics and list/detail APIs are preserved. No new SQL, RPC, dependency or mutation endpoint is introduced.

`CommercialAnalytics` adds presentation-ready integer-cent totals, previous-period changes (NULL percentage when the previous base is zero), channel breakdowns, product/category rankings, cost coverage and data-quality counts. React only formats and renders values. Current/future periods are explicitly marked as unclosed, so a partial selected month is not represented as a matched-days comparison with a full previous month. January1900, the supported lower boundary, has no previous comparison rather than reading an invalid year.

Confirmed commercial sales include recorded shipping and require the existing exact approved-ledger reconciliation. Purchases are recognized acquisitions, not cash outflows. Registered sales, confirmed sales, payments attributed to sale month, dated receipts in cash month and undated receipts attributed to historical sale month remain separate. Amounts by method come from order_payments, including detailed debit/credit/QR methods only when actually recorded. Order counts across mixed payment methods are not additive. Refund net remains uncertified and visibly disclosed.

Rankings use confirmed nonexcluded sales and the original product/category name snapshots. Product identity is product_id; missing identities are kept as distinct line groups instead of guessed by name. Categories are grouped by stored category name because there is no immutable category-ID snapshot. Rank quantities remain observable on line/header discrepancies, but monetary sums from those lines are omitted and explicitly marked; no historical discounts are reconstructed. The displayed top10 is labelled as a ranking, not an implicit limit on monthly totals.

Gross margin is partial: only reconciled confirmed lines with positive stored snapshots bearing the published transaction cost policy. Coverage uses merchandise revenue, excluding recorded shipping. NULL or unverified/zero costs do not become zero COGS, and current product costs are never selected. Snapshot provenance is not independent verification of supplier invoices; fees, operating expenses and uncertain refunds are not net profit. With no covered lines the margin is NULL/No disponible. The dashboard discloses unpriced revenue, unknown cost lines, imputed purchase dates, unverified date provenance, reconciliation issues and exact known synthetic exclusions.

The monthly reader retains the existing30-second budget and explicit page ceilings. It reads the full compact authorized dataset once (including older sales needed to recognize later receipts), not one query per product or period. Larger-volume optimization by server-side period union is deferred; time-budget exhaustion fails visibly instead of returning truncated metrics. Multi-SELECT consistency is best-effort, not an atomic global snapshot. No new Production verification is claimed by local fixtures.
